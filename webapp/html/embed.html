Visual perception is one of the most complex processes the human brain performs, handling approximately 10 million bits of data per second. Yet this remarkable system can be reliably fooled by surprisingly simple geometric patterns and color arrangements, revealing deep truths about how consciousness constructs reality from raw sensory input.

Artists, magicians, architects, and neuroscientists have all exploited the gap between what our eyes detect and what our brain interprets. From Renaissance forced-perspective paintings to modern UX design, understanding illusions has driven breakthroughs in medicine, technology, and creative expression — proving that seeing is rarely believing.
